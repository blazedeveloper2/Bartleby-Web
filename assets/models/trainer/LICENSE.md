# The trainer

The figure in the Workout app's 3D exercise animations is **Male_Adult_01** from the
[Microsoft Rocketbox](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox) avatar library,
converted for the web: the mesh baked into its A-pose and packed into `trainer.bin`
(layout in `trainer.json`), face bones folded into the head, textures resized, and
the shirt and shorts recoloured into training kit. The scripts that did it are
described in [tools/howto/README.md](../../../tools/howto/README.md).

Gonzalez-Franco et al., *The Rocketbox library and the utility of freely available
rigged avatars*, Frontiers in Virtual Reality (2020), doi:10.3389/frvir.2020.561558.
